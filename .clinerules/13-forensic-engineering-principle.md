# Forensic Engineering Principle

Prefer evidence over interpretation.

When investigating a discrepancy:

OBSERVE
→ REPRODUCE
→ ISOLATE
→ CLASSIFY
→ PROVE
→ MODIFY
→ VERIFY

Do not:

ASSUME
→ PATCH
→ RERUN
→ INTERPRET THE NEW RESULT AS PROOF

A successful result after a speculative modification does not prove that the modification was the root-cause fix.

The agent must establish causality before claiming a fix.
